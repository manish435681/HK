Safety

Do’s

  1. In case of fire / anything unusual on electric traction equipment or wires, inform the Station Controller / Manager.
  2. Extinguish fire by special extinguishers (carbon tetrachloride or carbon dioxide type, if available).
  3. Ensure no water jet is directed at the fire under any circumstances.
  4. Before taking up work on a line running parallel to 25 kV AC lines, the line shall be earthed on both sides. Ensure the distance between the two earths used for protection does not exceed 1 km.
  5. Keep clear of the track and avoid contact with the rails when an electric train is within 250 m.
  6. Take special care when carrying long pipes, poles or ladders so they do not come in contact with, or within 2 m of, live OHE.
  7. Cleaning work other than surface cleaning (i.e. of beams, pillars etc.) should be done during block period only.
  8. Whenever washing or cleaning using water jets is done, take appropriate power block.
  9. Report cases of electric shock from contact with 25 kV AC traction equipment immediately to TPC.
Safety

Don’t

  1. Do not approach within 2 m of any traction wires or live equipment.
  2. Do not work on or near traction wires or any live equipment unless they are made dead, earthed and shut down notices / permit to work obtained.
  3. Do not enter any switching station or remote control centre unless specially permitted.
  4. Do not touch a person in contact with live traction wires. Remove the body only after power supply is switched off and earthed.
  5. Do not touch any traction wire hanging from the mast or fallen on the ground, and do not allow anyone else to touch it.
  6. Avoid cleaning with conducting materials like aluminium / steel rods at all times when power block is not availed.
  7. Do not lift or raise your tools towards traction wires.
  8. Do not damage the plinth continuity, connection to BEC, OPC and handrail continuity.
  9. Do not use steel tape, metallic tape or tape with woven metal reinforcement in an electrified area.
  10. Do not forget to give artificial respiration to the victim as per the procedure laid down in the shock treatment charts.
  11. Metallic telescopic rods are prohibited for use in NMRC stations.
Safety

Cleaning Near 25 kV Traction: Permits

  • Platform roof ceiling: non-operational hours only, with shadow power block and permit to work (PTW) from the Station Controller.
  • Lighting fixtures near platform arches and the 25 kV AC system: shadow power block and PTW from operations staff.
  • Track plinth: non-operational hours only, special permit from NMRC authorised representative. One or both rails may carry traction return current.
  • Do not disturb track arrangement or traction, E&M, signalling or communication equipment near the track.
  • Platform drains in operating hours: only with permission of NMRC authorised person and safety signage.

Passenger safetyCleaning and housekeeping works in the stations are to be carried out with special care during commercial hours, so that passenger movement is not affected, no accident is caused to personnel or passengers, and all areas of the station always present a perfectly clean appearance. BreaksEach shift includes a one-hour break. Shifts and breaks may be staggered to ensure continuous availability of cleaning and housekeeping personnel.

Operations

Special Conditions for Cleaning

Cleaning reagents

Environment-friendly reagents / detergents to be used to the extent possible. They should be free from chemical reactions, odourless, and must not affect commuters, employees, materials or equipment. Employer’s approval must be obtained before use.

Submission within 3 days of award

Final list of eco-friendly reagents / detergents / chemicals with technical details and test reports. Also details of disinfectants and pest / rodent control chemicals with full technical details, for approval.

Operations

Cleaning & Housekeeping Machinery / Equipment

  1. Machines and equipment shall have adequate capacity so that all operations are comfortably completed during non-operational hours and shift hours.
  2. Machinery used in passenger areas (circulating area, concourse, staircases, platforms etc.) shall be battery operated. The contractor must use his own battery chargers.
  3. Machinery shall be energy efficient, draw current in proportion to machine capacity, and be well maintained.
  4. Only adequately trained personnel shall use the machines and equipment.
